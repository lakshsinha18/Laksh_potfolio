PrototypeWeather

VERSAAI: regime-aware weather forecast correction

  • Corrects bias in raw NWP model output by classifying the weather regime, then blending expert models with Mixture-of-Experts soft gating.
  • Produces explainable heavy-rain probability and forecast-confidence scores for 24 districts across 6 meteorological regions of India.
  • Includes an interactive dashboard with forecast map, risk ranking, confidence and regime analysis, verification and automated advisories.

Demonstration prototype. All verification uses synthetic data.
